## Library
The library is open Monday to Friday 8:30 to 22:00 and weekends 9:00 to 17:00, with extended hours during exam periods. Students can borrow up to 10 items for 14 days and renew online. Online databases (IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, Scopus) are available off campus through single sign-on.

## IT services
Every student and staff member gets a university email account, Microsoft 365 apps and campus Wi-Fi access. Forgotten passwords can be reset through the self-service password portal. For other IT problems, log a ticket with the IT Service Desk through the portal or visit the help desk on Level 3.

## Student services
Student Services handles enrolment letters, student ID cards, accommodation advice, clubs and societies, and general enquiries. Requests for official letters are made through the student portal and are ready within 3 working days.

## Getting to campus
The campus is next to the LRT Bukit Jalil station area and is served by a free shuttle bus from nearby residences during semester weeks.
