## Intakes
Undergraduate programmes have intakes several times a year, typically in January, March, May, July, September and November. Foundation and diploma programmes follow a similar schedule. Postgraduate taught programmes usually start in February, June and September. Check the programme page for the exact dates of the next intake.

## How to apply
1. Choose a programme and check its entry requirements.
2. Apply online through the APU application portal and upload certified copies of your academic results, identity card or passport, and a passport-size photo.
3. Pay the non-refundable application fee.
4. Receive a conditional or unconditional offer letter, usually within 5 working days of a complete application.
5. Accept the offer and pay the registration fee to secure your place.

## Entry requirements: Computer Science degree
- STPM: minimum 2 principal passes (Grade C, GP 2.0) including Mathematics, plus a credit in Mathematics at SPM.
- A-Level: minimum 2 passes including Mathematics.
- APU Foundation in Computing: pass with a minimum CGPA of 2.0.
- Diploma in a related field: minimum CGPA of 2.5.
- English: IELTS 5.0 or equivalent for international applicants.
Requirements differ by programme; the programme page is the authoritative source.

## Scholarships
APU offers merit scholarships based on SPM, STPM, A-Level or equivalent results, sports and co-curricular scholarships, and need-based bursaries. Merit scholarships cover between 10% and 100% of tuition fees depending on results. Applications are made together with the programme application. Scholarships must be maintained by meeting a minimum CGPA each semester.
