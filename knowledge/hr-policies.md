## Annual leave
Full-time staff are entitled to annual leave based on length of service: 16 days per year for less than 2 years, 18 days for 2 to 5 years, and 22 days for more than 5 years. Leave is applied for through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance and approved by the line manager. Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Medical leave
Staff are entitled to 14 days of paid medical leave per year (18 days after 2 years of service, 22 days after 5 years), plus up to 60 days of hospitalisation leave. A medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 2 working days.

## Training and development
Each staff member has an annual development allowance for courses, conferences and certifications. Requests are submitted through the HR portal with a short justification and manager approval.

## Working arrangements
Administrative staff may request up to 1 day per week of remote work, subject to manager approval and service coverage.
