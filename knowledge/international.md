## Student visa process (EMGS)
International students need a Student Pass, applied for through Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS). APU submits the application on the student's behalf after the offer is accepted.
1. Accept the offer and pay the EMGS processing fee and the first semester fees.
2. Upload your passport (valid for at least 18 months), academic certificates, passport photo and a health declaration to the EMGS portal.
3. Wait for the Visa Approval Letter (VAL), typically 4 to 8 weeks.
4. Travel to Malaysia with the VAL and complete the medical screening within 7 days of arrival.
5. Submit your passport to APU so the Student Pass sticker can be endorsed.

## Renewing your Student Pass
Start the renewal at least 3 months before your pass expires. Renewal requires satisfactory attendance (at least 80%) and academic progress. Late renewal may incur penalties from the Immigration Department.

## Arrival support
The International Student Office offers airport pickup on request, an orientation week, help opening a bank account and a guide to accommodation near campus.
