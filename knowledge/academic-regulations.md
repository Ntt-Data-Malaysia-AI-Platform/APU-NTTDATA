## Attendance
Students must attend at least 80% of scheduled classes for each module. Students below 80% may be barred from the final examination. International students' attendance is also reported for Student Pass renewal.

## Missed examinations and extenuating circumstances
If you miss an examination or assessment deadline because of illness or another serious reason, submit an Extenuating Circumstances (EC) application through the student portal within 3 working days of the assessment. Attach evidence such as a medical certificate from a registered practitioner. If the EC is approved, you sit the assessment at the next opportunity without penalty and without the attempt being counted as a resit.

## Resits
A student who fails a module may resit the failed component once. The mark for a passed resit is capped at the minimum pass mark (40% for undergraduate modules). Resit fees apply. A second failure requires retaking the whole module.

## Results and appeals
Results are released through the student portal after the Examination Board meets, usually 4 to 6 weeks after the exam period. Appeals against a result must be submitted within 10 working days of release and must be based on procedural irregularity or unconsidered extenuating circumstances, not on disagreement with academic judgement.

## Student wellbeing and counselling
The Student Counselling Centre offers free, confidential counselling. Students can book online or walk in during office hours. In an emergency or if you feel unsafe, contact campus security immediately or call the Malaysian emergency number 999. The Befrienders Kuala Lumpur helpline (03-7627 2929) offers 24-hour emotional support.
