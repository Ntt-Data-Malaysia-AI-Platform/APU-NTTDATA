## PhD candidature and milestones
A full-time PhD normally takes 3 to 4 years (part-time 4 to 6). Candidates must pass the research proposal defence within 12 months of registration, submit an annual progress report signed by the main supervisor, and pass a mid-candidature review around the halfway point. Missing a progress report by more than 30 days places the candidate on monitored status.

## Supervision
Each PhD candidate has a main supervisor and at least one co-supervisor. Candidates should meet their supervisors at least once a month and record each meeting, with agreed actions, in the supervision log on the postgraduate portal. Changes of supervisor are requested through the Graduate School.

## Thesis submission and viva
Before submitting the thesis, a PhD candidate needs at least one article accepted in an indexed (Scopus or Web of Science) journal, a completed similarity check, and a notice of intention to submit 3 months ahead. The thesis is examined by one internal and two external examiners, followed by an oral examination (viva). Outcomes range from pass with minor corrections to resubmission.

## Funding and conference support
Graduate research assistantships pay a monthly stipend in return for up to 20 hours a week of research or teaching support. Candidates can apply to the Graduate School for a conference allowance once per year when presenting a paper; apply at least 6 weeks before travel with the acceptance letter.

## Master's dissertation and credits
Master's programmes by coursework require 42 credits, including a dissertation or capstone project. The dissertation proposal must be approved by the assigned supervisor before data collection starts. Part-time students attend weekend classes and usually finish in 2 to 3 years.

## Deferment and leave of absence
Postgraduate students can apply to defer up to two semesters for work, health or family reasons. Apply through the postgraduate portal before the semester starts, with supporting documents. International students must check with the International Student Office first, because deferment can affect the Student Pass.
