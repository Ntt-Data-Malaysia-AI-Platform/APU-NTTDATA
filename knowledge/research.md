## Funding sources
Researchers can apply for internal seed grants, national grants such as the Fundamental Research Grant Scheme (FRGS) from the Ministry of Higher Education, and industry-funded projects. The Research Office publishes a calendar of open calls and internal deadlines. Internal deadlines are usually 2 to 4 weeks before the funder's deadline to allow review.

## Writing a strong proposal
A proposal normally includes: a problem statement with evidence of the gap, clear objectives, a methodology, expected outcomes and their impact, a timeline with milestones, a budget with justification, and the team's track record. The Research Office offers proposal reviews and mock panels before submission.

## Research ethics
All research involving human participants, personal data or animals requires approval from the Research Ethics Committee before data collection begins. Submit the ethics application with the protocol, consent forms, data management plan and instruments. Low-risk applications are reviewed within 2 weeks; full committee review takes up to 6 weeks.

## Data management
Research data must be stored on university-approved storage, never on personal cloud accounts. Personal data must be handled in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). Data management plans must state where data is stored, who can access it and how long it is kept.

## Publishing
Researchers are encouraged to publish in Scopus- or Web of Science-indexed venues. Article processing charges for open access may be supported by the Research Office on application. Use your APU affiliation and ORCID iD on all publications.
