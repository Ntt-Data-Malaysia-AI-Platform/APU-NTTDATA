## Tuition fees
Tuition fees are charged per semester. As an illustration, a three-year Computer Science degree for Malaysian students is around RM 30,000 to RM 35,000 per year, and higher for international students. Fees for each programme are published in the fee schedule on the programme page. Fees are reviewed annually.

## Payment deadlines
Semester fees are due within 14 days of the semester invoice date. Students can pay by online banking (FPX), credit or debit card, or bank transfer. A late payment charge applies after the due date, and students with outstanding fees may be barred from sitting final examinations or receiving results.

## Instalment plans
Students facing difficulty can apply to the Finance Office for an instalment plan before the due date. Plans normally split the semester fee into up to three monthly payments.

## PTPTN and sponsorship
Malaysian students can apply for PTPTN education loans. Students sponsored by an employer or agency should submit the sponsorship letter to the Finance Office so invoices can be issued to the sponsor.

## Refund policy
- Withdrawal before the programme starts: registration fee is non-refundable; tuition paid is refunded in full.
- Withdrawal within the first 2 weeks of the semester: 50% of the semester tuition is refunded.
- Withdrawal after 2 weeks: no refund of that semester's tuition.
Refund requests are submitted through the student portal with a completed withdrawal form and are processed within 30 working days.
