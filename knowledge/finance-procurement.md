## Procurement thresholds
- Up to RM 2,000: purchase with one quotation and head of department approval.
- RM 2,001 to RM 10,000: three written quotations and approval from the head of department and Finance.
- Above RM 10,000: formal tender or three quotations with a comparison sheet, approved by the Procurement Committee.
All purchases must be raised as a purchase requisition in the finance system before the order is placed.

## Expense claims
Staff submit expense claims through the finance system within 30 days of the expense, with original receipts attached. Claims are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll after approval.

## Travel
Overseas travel for conferences requires approval from the Dean and the Research or HR office at least 4 weeks before travel. Economy class is standard for flights under 8 hours.
